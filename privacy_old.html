This policy explains information processing in our app, currently called TowerClimber, and its website. You can play without signing in with Google. A guest account still has an identifier; it is not anonymous to the services handling that identifier.

1. Who is responsible

Our existing notice identifies Matej Samardjiev, trading as All Star Studios, North Macedonia, as the operator responsible for the app's processing of personal information. Contact support.allstarstudios@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests.

Before publication: confirm this legal identity, add the appropriate business/contact address, and identify any representative required in the markets where the app is offered.

2. Accounts, sign-in and sessions

When the app first connects successfully to our account service, it creates a guest account with a random player ID. We store account type, creation and login times, and associated progress. Before a connection is available, gameplay and pending actions may exist only on your phone.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies a credential that our service verifies. The sign-in SDK and credential can contain basic profile information. Our account-linking code stores Google's stable account identifier linked to your player ID; it does not save your Google email, name or profile photo as part of that link. We can still receive your email if you contact support.

Linking a new Google identity to a guest keeps that player account. Signing into an identity already linked to another account restores that account instead; it does not automatically merge two players' progress. Sign-in and account switching require connectivity.

The app keeps session credentials in the phone's secure credential storage. Our server keeps protected refresh-session records, including a one-way hash of the refresh verifier, expiry and revocation information. Short-lived access credentials are signed rather than saved as plaintext tokens in a token database. These credentials authenticate requests to your game account.

3. Progress and local storage

We process gold and character-token balances, owned characters, high scores and run statistics, check-in and reward claims, pack results, purchase entitlements and balance-change histories. These support cloud saves, reward delivery, support and abuse prevention.

Your phone stores progress, selected character, tutorial state, sound settings, pending actions and cached legal documents and acknowledgements. Sound settings stay on that phone. When connectivity returns, the app sends queued gameplay, pack and unlock actions and relevant progress to the backend for validation and reconciliation. Local storage and pending queues have limits; data loss, account replacement or a prolonged period without successful sync can prevent some progress from reaching the server.

4. Purchases and rewarded ads

Where purchases are available, the app store processes payment and RevenueCat handles purchase verification and entitlement information. The integration does not send us your payment card number or security code.

RevenueCat receives an app-specific identifier and, after identification, your player ID. It handles purchase history, receipts or purchase tokens, products, store/platform details and transaction information. Our backend retains purchase-event messages and processing results. Depending on the event, these messages can include player identifiers and aliases, transaction references, products, entitlements, prices/currency, dates, country codes, and refund or transfer details. We therefore retain more than just the product and purchase date.

For verified rewarded ads, the app sends your player ID and a reward-verification value to Google. Its verification callback lets our server check the reward. We retain claim placement, reward, status, verification value, network transaction reference and relevant times to deliver rewards and prevent duplicate claims.

5. Advertising and your choices

The app uses Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Requests are configured as non-personalised. This does not mean no personal information is processed: Google's SDK can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device/advertising identifiers, ad interactions and performance information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Processing depends on platform, settings, consent choices and the ad service's configuration.

The app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. When its privacy-options form is available, reopen it through Profile → Ad privacy options. Device advertising/privacy settings offer additional controls. A policy acknowledgement or Terms acceptance is not consent to optional advertising. Non-personalised requests do not replace consent required by law.

Release condition: correct and verify consent-error handling and application of changed choices to subsequent ad requests. The current code can allow requests after a consent error and does not reliably refresh permission after the privacy form closes.

Remove Ads suppresses automatic interstitial displays. You may still choose rewarded ads. It does not erase provider records or necessarily stop SDK initialization or ad preloading. Read Google's explanation of data from partner apps.

6. Technical records, website and support

Our backend processes IP addresses to handle and rate-limit requests. Security, purchase, reward and error logs can contain player or transaction identifiers. Hosting/delivery services may process IP addresses, URLs, request times, browser/device details and diagnostic records. Information absent from a player table can still appear in logs or provider records.

No separate gameplay-analytics or crash-reporting SDK is integrated in the reviewed app. This does not exclude AdMob diagnostics and measurement, RevenueCat purchase reporting, store reports, or server and hosting logs.

Netlify hosts the website. Some existing pages load Google Fonts and a QR-code script from Cloudflare cdnjs, sending requests and technical information to those providers. The site's own source contains no analytics tracker or intentional cookie/local-storage feature; third-party services and your browser have their own request and storage practices.

Our Gmail support inbox receives your address, messages and attachments. Correspondence may be associated with the player ID or transaction you ask us to investigate. Send only necessary information, never passwords, session tokens or full card details.

The reviewed gameplay does not request access to contacts, calendar, precise GPS location, microphone, camera or a personal photo library. This is not a claim that advertising, store or hosting providers process no other information.

7. Purposes and legal grounds

Where a legal basis is required, the proposed grounds are:

  • Providing the service: account/session handling, progress, rewards and purchase delivery as necessary for our agreement with you or steps you request.
  • Security and support: proportionate logs, verification, abuse prevention and dispute investigation for our legitimate interests in a secure, functioning service, subject to your rights and the required balancing assessment.
  • Legal obligations: relevant information needed to comply with an applicable law or lawful request, not a blanket justification for keeping every purchase field forever.
  • Optional processing: separate consent where required for advertising identifiers or similar device access; other grounds only where the law permits them.
  • Legal notices: version acknowledgements for accountability and evidence of the notice presented and agreement made, including handling disputes.

Information necessary for an online feature is needed to provide that feature. Declining optional advertising consent is separate from agreeing to the Terms. The policy popup does not waive your rights or authorize unrelated processing.

9. Retention

Account, progress, inventory and associated gameplay history remain in the active database until deletion; there is no automatic inactive-account expiry. Legal acknowledgements also remain linked to the player until account deletion.

Default scheduled cleanup removes audit entries and processed sync receipts older than 90 days. Expired/revoked refresh-session records become eligible once more than 30 days old from creation. These are code defaults, not verified production settings.

Purchase-event messages have no automatic deletion period. Some purchase and audit records remain after account deletion and can still contain identifiers. Support mail, infrastructure logs, provider records and backups have separate arrangements.

Before publication: approve and implement proportionate retention for purchase fields, support mail, infrastructure logs and backups; verify cleanup and provider settings; replace these review notes with actual periods or specific criteria. Necessary legal retention must be limited to relevant information and the applicable obligation or claim period. Unbounded retention is not justified merely by mentioning it here.

10. Account deletion

While online, open Profile → Delete Account and confirm. Your game session authenticates the request. On success the backend deletes the player, sign-in links, refresh sessions, balances and ledgers, characters, statistics, reward/pack history, entitlements and legal acknowledgements associated with that account.

The app then clears that device's account session/progress and can create a new guest. Device-wide preferences can remain. It does not remotely erase copies on other offline devices or instantly revoke every previously issued short-lived access credential. Deleted database records cannot be recovered by those credentials.

Retained purchase messages and audit metadata can still contain identifiers even when the direct player link is removed; they are not necessarily anonymous. Deletion does not delete your Google/store account, request a refund, or automatically erase RevenueCat's customer record. Eligible purchase restoration is separate from recovery of deleted progress.

Without reinstalling, email support.allstarstudios@gmail.com with “Account deletion request.” Include your player ID if available; it locates the account but alone does not prove ownership. Proportionate additional verification may be needed. A Google email alone cannot be matched to an email field in our account-linking database.

Release condition: establish support ownership verification, processor deletion handling and backup/retained-record rules. Do not present the current operation as complete erasure from every provider and backup.

11. Rights and security

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability of eligible information. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact support; we may verify identity proportionately.

Applicable legal response periods apply. Where the GDPR applies, the normal period is one month; permitted extensions require explanation. You can complain to the relevant supervisory authority, including North Macedonia's Personal Data Protection Agency or your competent local authority. Each right is subject to its legal conditions.

Access controls and protected session storage reduce unauthorized access; no system guarantees absolute security. Contact support about suspected misuse. Do not send session credentials as proof of ownership.

12. Children

The existing audience statement says the app is not directed at children under 13. The current app does not verify age or offer parental-consent registration. Contact support if a child has provided information inappropriately so the account and relevant provider processing can be assessed and addressed.

Before publication: confirm audience, store declarations, local age rules and treatment of younger users. A policy statement is not an age gate or proof that children's information is not collected.

13. Versions and acknowledgements

The legal-notice system records document type/version, an event ID, device-reported time and server receipt time. Offline acknowledgements await synchronization with the appropriate player. Device times are not verified proof of when a person read the document.

After release, current Privacy Policy and Terms URLs stay the same, with separate archived versions. The app checks requirements when connected. Required updates prompt for the changed document; updates detected during a climb are deferred until it finishes. Older offline acknowledgements never satisfy a newer version.

Linking without changing player ID retains acknowledgement history. Another account has its own history; an offline device without that history may ask again. A policy update alone does not sign you out. Acknowledging this notice confirms receipt; agreeing to the Terms of Service is a distinct action even in one popup. Neither replaces consent required for optional processing.
