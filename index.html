How it plays

You build the tower.
The climber does the climbing.

You never move the character directly. You decide where the next block goes, and the climber takes whatever path you just made possible.

Place, don't steer

Drag a piece onto the grid. Every valid placement sends the climber as high as your structure allows.

The water never stops

Build too slowly and it catches you. Build carelessly and you wall your own climber in. Both lose.

Collect the crew

Earn gold as you climb, open packs, and unlock new characters from Common all the way to Mythic.

Privacy

We collect very little

No signup, and no name attached to your progress.

You can play the entire game as an anonymous guest, with a randomly generated account ID. Signing in is optional and exists so your progress survives changing phones.

Deleting your account

In the game, go to Profile › Delete Account. It takes effect immediately.

Prefer to ask us? Email support.allstarstudios@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request.

If you signed in with Google, send it from that account so we can identify you. If you played as a guest, include the Player ID from the Profile screen. We action requests within 30 days.

What gets erased, and what we are required to keep, is set out in the privacy policy.
