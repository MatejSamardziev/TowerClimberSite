Privacy Policy

All Star Studios · Last updated 18 September 2026

The short version. You can play TowerClimber as a guest, without giving us your name or your email address. Your account is a randomly generated ID. We never see your payment details. We show ads, and we ask our ad provider for non-personalised ads. We do not sell your data.

This policy explains what TowerClimber collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the TowerClimber mobile game on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and this website.

Who we are

TowerClimber is published under the name All Star Studios, which is a trading name of Matej Samardjiev, an individual developer based in North Macedonia. Matej Samardjiev is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR and equivalent laws.

For anything in this policy, contact support.allstarstudios@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

When you first open the game we create a guest account for you automatically. That account is a randomly generated identifier. It is not derived from your device, your phone number, or anything about you. Alongside it we store the type of account, when it was created, and when it was last used.

The game never asks you for your name or your email address, and our servers do not store either one.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It exists so your progress survives losing or changing your phone, and it is required before you can make a purchase, so purchases can be tied to your account and supported if something goes wrong.

We use Google Sign In to obtain a stable identifier for your Google account, and we store that identifier so we can recognise you next time. We do not store your Google email address, your name, or your profile picture.

Your game progress

So your progress can follow you across devices, we store your high score and run statistics, your gold and character token balances, which characters you own, your daily reward streak, and your pack opening history. We also keep a record of changes to your balances, so that if something goes wrong we can see what happened and put it right.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the app store you bought from, Google Play or the Apple App Store, through a service called RevenueCat. Your card details never reach us and we never see them.

RevenueCat processes what it needs to verify a purchase, which may include your TowerClimber account identifier, device and platform information, the store receipt or purchase token, the product bought, and transaction details and dates. What we store on our own servers is the product, a transaction reference, and when it happened, so we can credit your account and support you if a purchase goes wrong.

Advertising

TowerClimber shows ads through Google AdMob. We configure AdMob to request non-personalised ads, meaning ads are not targeted using a profile of your interests.

Even for non-personalised ads, Google may access your device's advertising identifier and general device and network information in order to cap how often you see the same ad, to detect fraud, and to report aggregate performance. That processing is Google's, under Google's own privacy terms.

Buying Remove Ads stops ads being shown to you in the game.

When you contact us

If you email our support address, we receive whatever you send, including your email address and anything you choose to put in the message. We keep that correspondence for as long as we need it to deal with your query and to handle any follow up, then delete it. We do not add it to your game account or use it for marketing.

Technical information

Our servers see your IP address while handling your requests, and use it briefly to limit how often requests can be made from one place. This is anti abuse only. We do not build a profile from it and do not store it against your account.

This website

This website is hosted by Netlify. When you visit it, Netlify may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser and device information, the pages you request and the time of each request, in order to deliver and secure the site. We do not run analytics on this website, and we do not intentionally set cookies or store information in your browser.

What we do not collect

  • Your postal address or phone number.
  • Your precise location. The game never asks for GPS or location permission. Our providers may infer an approximate location, such as country or city, from your IP address, for advertising, fraud prevention and running the service.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or calendar.
  • Microphone or camera access. The game does not request either.
  • Anything you type in the game, beyond what is needed to run it.

Why we are allowed to hold it

If you are in the UK, EU, or somewhere with comparable law: we process your account and progress data because it is necessary to provide the game you asked for. We retain purchase and transaction records where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, and because we have a legitimate interest in supporting purchases, preventing fraud and resolving disputes. Anti abuse rests on the same legitimate interest in protecting the game.

For advertising: where the law requires consent, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before advertising technologies use device identifiers or similar storage, and you can change your choice at any time. Where consent is not required, advertising rests on our legitimate interest in funding the game.

Where that happens we rely on the transfer safeguards available under applicable law, such as the EU to US Data Privacy Framework where a provider participates in it, or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and progress for as long as you use the game.

To delete your account, open the game and go to Profile › Delete Account. It takes effect immediately.

You can also email support.allstarstudios@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, or include the Player ID shown on the game's Profile screen. We action requests within 30 days.

When you delete your account we permanently delete it and the game data attached to it, including your gold and token balances, the characters you own, your statistics and high score, your daily reward state and your pack history. Any linked sign in is disconnected and every session is signed out. This cannot be undone, and anything you have purchased goes with it.

We keep purchase and transaction records where we need them for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or another legal obligation, along with a minimal record that a deletion was carried out. These records are detached from your TowerClimber account when it is deleted and are no longer linked to an active game account. The app stores hold the billing details, not us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to complain to your data protection regulator. Email us at support.allstarstudios@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Because a guest account holds no name or email address, we may need the Player ID shown in the game to identify the account your request relates to.

Children

TowerClimber is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete the account.

Security

All traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign in tokens are stored in your device's secure storage, and the tokens we hold on our servers are stored hashed rather than in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold, which by design is very little.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
